# Propeller

The selected propeller for this motor and our 15t boat is a Gori, 3-blade, folding propeller, ø 16.5", 12" pitch, with overdrive.
The Gori team recommanded a 13" pitch... but we got a brand new 12" pitch for half the price.

A lower pitch means a lower top speed (not a problem) and should be more reactive while manoeuvering.
