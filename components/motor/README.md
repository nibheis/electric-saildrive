# Electric motor

The electric motor has been choosen based on the following criterions:

1. 48 V
1. Speed compatible with off the shelf saildrives 
1. Efficiency at the working speed
1. Integrated water cooling
1. Integrated COS/SIN encoder

The ENGIRO 205W 04013 has been selected in its "SHE" variant (female spline adaptor) to reduce the space of the coupling between the motor and the saildrive (see the "shaft" component).
