# Throttle

This trottle (Flexball 4103) has been selected because:

1. It is of good quality and can live outside in the rain/sun/sea
1. It has a button on the top of the lever

The button goes to the motor controller and is used to switch between 'max power mode' (default) and 'eco mode'.
The 'eco mode' is a mode where both the motor and the controller are both withing their limits of continuous work.
